Questions

Quick answers

Is it really free?

Yes. Reading notes, PDFs and the AI helper (small daily limit) are free. A free student account adds quizzes, progress, streaks and the leaderboard.

Do I need to log in?

No, not to read notes or download PDFs. The Ask AI button on this page also works without an account. Log in only for quizzes, progress, notes and email updates.

How does the AI helper work, and can it be wrong?

It answers study questions in Bangla or English and explains notes simply. Visitors get a few questions a day, students get more. AI can make mistakes, so always check important facts in the notes.

Can I use it on my phone and offline?

Yes. Install it like an app (see “Get the app” below). Opened pages and PDFs you downloaded work without internet.

Where do the notes come from?

Questions come from past papers (2021–2025) with simple answers written for the exam script. Teachers’ suggestions are marked with stars. New notes are added from time to time.

I found a mistake or want a topic added.

Please tell us in the Feedback box below. Every message is read, and fixes are published quickly.
