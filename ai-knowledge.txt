ABOUT THE SITE
AGRi Elite Arena (formerly AGRi ELITE NOTES) is a free exam-preparation website for HSTU (Hajee Mohammad Danesh Science and Technology University) Faculty of Agriculture, Level 1 Semester 1 (L-1 S-1), batch 26. Address: https://agri-elite-arena.pages.dev . Owner and developer: Mahfuz R. Shanto. It is free; no payment is ever needed.
What students get: (1) course notes pages with year-wise question analysis (2022-2025), exam-style answers with a Bangla summary (sarkotha) after each answer, instant search, topic tabs, Suggestion-only and Asked-2x filters, finished-reading ticks and a last-night revision box; (2) PDFs: Question Analysis, Answers, year-wise Question Papers and year-wise Q&A, opened in the built-in reader with Back, resume-page and download; (3) Student Portal (portal.html) after free sign-up (email or Google): dashboard, bookmarks, practice exams, timed exams, Mock Exam, Exam Arena (MCQ), flashcards and your own cards, AI study-plan generator, study planner, weekly goal, focus timer, revision tools, leaderboard, study groups, badges, forum, profile photo, notification settings; (4) Ask AI helper (this assistant) on every page; (5) exam routine with live countdown and calendar (.ics) download; (6) reminders: push notification and email (new notes, exam in 3 days, exam tomorrow night, weekly progress email on Fridays), controllable in Profile; (7) install as an app (PWA) from the browser menu; (8) feedback form at the bottom of the home page; (9) campus-life photos and study videos; (10) dark mode button, back-to-top arrow, Continue where you left off.
How to: Sign up -> portal.html -> Register or Continue with Google. Forgot password -> portal.html -> Forgot password. Install app -> browser menu -> Install app / Add to Home screen. PDF -> Downloads section on the home page or inside each course page -> Read online or Download. Send feedback -> bottom of the home page. Report a mistake in a note -> use the feedback form.
Team: Owner Mahfuz R. Shanto. Admins: Arpon Mizan, Armanul Islam, Salauddin Rimon, Uthsab Roy. Editors: Abrar Jahin, Tanvir Tonmoy. Moderator: Robiul Ratul. Admin panel is only for roled team members.
Courses live now: AGF 101 (Fundamentals of Environmental Science), SSC 101 (Introductory Soil Science), SOC 101 (Rural Sociology). AGN 101 is coming soon.
