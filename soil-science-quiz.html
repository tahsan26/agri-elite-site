Q2. Sterilization, agents ও bacteria এর উদাহরণ 2.5

Sterilization: The act or process of destroying all forms of life (including spores) is known as sterilization.

  • Physical agents: dry heat, moist heat, filtration, radiation.
  • Chemical agents: alcohol, chlorine, formaldehyde, ethylene oxide.

Examples:

  • Motile: Pseudomonas, Rhizobium
  • Non-motile: Sarcina, Corynebacterium
  • Gram positive: Bacillus subtilis, Staphylococcus aureus
  • Gram negative: Escherichia coli, Salmonella typhi

Q2. কেন সাধারণত 0–15 cm গভীরতা থেকে মাটি নেওয়া হয়? 1.0

Q1. Composite soil sample; morphological description 2.0

Composite soil sample: It is a mixture of individual samples collected from different spots at the same depth of the selected field and well mixed together to form a single homogenised representative sample.

Morphological description to be recorded: নোটে আংশিক location, site (land type/topography), depth of sampling, soil colour, texture, structure, moisture condition, drainage and previous crop or land use.

Q2. Agar, hot water bath, core sampler, hollow ground slide এর function 2.0
  • Agar: a solidifying agent that makes culture media solid. প্রশ্নে “sugar” ছাপা, আসলে agar (নিশ্চিত)
  • Hot water bath: heats the soil–water suspension gently to disperse aggregates and expel trapped air in particle density determination (15 minutes).
  • Core sampler: collects an undisturbed soil core of known volume for bulk density.
  • Hollow ground slide: a slide with a concave depression that holds the hanging drop for the bacterial motility test.
Q3. Autoclaving; time, pressure, temperature 2.0

Autoclaving: Sterilization of materials with saturated steam under pressure to kill microorganisms and their spores. An autoclave is the device used.

  • Temperature: 121 °C
  • Pressure: 15 psi
  • Time: 15–30 minutes

Moist heat is better than dry heat because heat penetrates rapidly in a moist environment, so less time is needed.

Q4. Gram staining; crystal violet ও mordant এর কাজ 2.0

Gram staining: A differential staining method developed by the Danish bacteriologist Hans Christian Gram in 1884. It separates bacteria into Gram positive (violet) and Gram negative (red).

  • Crystal violet: the primary (basic) dye; stains all bacterial cells violet.
  • Mordant (iodine solution): increases the affinity of the basic dye to the bacterial cell wall and intensifies the colour of the stain.
  • Then alcohol (decolourizing agent) and safranin (counter stain, red) are used.

Reason of difference: thick peptidoglycan layer in Gram positive; Gram negative has an outer lipid membrane that dissolves in alcohol, so it loses the violet colour.

Q5. BD 1.12 g/cm³, porosity 60% হলে particle density কত? 2.0

Porosity (%) = (1 − BD/PD) × 100

60 = (1 − 1.12/PD) × 100 → 1.12/PD = 1 − 0.60 = 0.40

PD = 1.12 ÷ 0.40 = 2.8 g/cm³

Mid term · 3 × 1

Q1. Define particle density, bulk density, rock, mineral 1.0
  • Particle density: mass per unit volume of soil solids, excluding pore space (PD = mass of dry soil ÷ volume of soil solids, g/cm³).
  • Bulk density: mass per unit volume of soil including pore space (BD = mass of oven-dry soil ÷ bulk volume of soil, g/cm³).
  • Rock: a mixture of minerals whose physical and chemical properties depend on the minerals present in it.
  • Mineral: a natural inorganic substance which has definite physical and chemical properties.
Q2. কোন জায়গা থেকে মাটি নেওয়া উচিত নয়? 1.0
  • Near field boundaries
  • Near trees and large plants
  • Near irrigation channels
  • Waterlogged wet areas
  • Places near roads and buildings
Q3. PD ও BD নির্ণয়ের পদ্ধতি ও মান 1.0
  • Particle density: volumetric flask method, pycnometer method.
  • Bulk density: paraffined clod method, core sampler method.
  • PD: mineral soil 2.60–2.75, organic soil 1.2–1.7 g/cm³.
  • BD: mineral soil 1.0–1.8, organic soil ≈ 0.5 g/cm³.

Seven methods of determining PD and BD; values 1.0

  • PD: 1. volumetric flask method, 2. pycnometer (density bottle) method, 3. water displacement method

Oven ও autoclave এর temperature, pressure, time; কী কী sterilize করা হয় 1.5

Autoclave: 121 °C, 15 psi, 15–30 minutes (culture media, water, instruments). Hot air oven: about 160 °C for 2 hours নোটে নেই (dry glassware like petri dish, test tube, flask, pipette).

Other functions: auger, iodine solution, petri dish, sieve, desiccator, autoclave 2.5
নোটে নেই
  • Auger: to collect soil samples from the desired depth.
  • Iodine solution: mordant in Gram staining.
  • Petri dish: to hold solid medium for bacterial culture.
  • Sieve: to remove stones and roots and get uniform fine soil (2 mm).
  • Desiccator: to cool dried samples without absorbing moisture.
  • Autoclave: to sterilize media and instruments.
  • Glass bead: ক্লাসে কী বলা হয়েছে তা মিলিয়ে নিও, আমি নিশ্চিত নই।
Culture media; motility of bacteria 2.0

Culture media: nutrient materials, liquid or solid, prepared for growing microorganisms in the laboratory. Examples: YMB (Yeast Mannitol Broth, liquid) and YMA (Yeast Mannitol Agar, solid). Mannitol is the carbohydrate source; yeast supplies amino acids, growth factors and vitamin B complex; agar solidifies the medium.

Motility: the ability of bacteria to move by themselves, mostly by flagella. It is tested by the hanging drop method on a hollow ground slide. Brownian movement is only back and forth vibration; true motility shows directional movement.

PD সবসময় BD এর চেয়ে বেশি কেন? 0.5

Particle density considers only the volume of solids, while bulk density considers total volume including pore space. Since total volume is always larger, BD is lower than PD. They would be equal only if there were no pore space, which never happens in soil. High BD means compact soil, low porosity and poor root growth.

Soil sampling কী? ৫টি physical ও ৫টি chemical property 2.0

Soil sampling: the process of collecting a small volume of soil from the field and its processing for subsequent mechanical and chemical analysis.

নোটে নেই Physical: texture, structure, bulk density, porosity, water holding capacity. Chemical: pH, organic matter, nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium (also EC, CEC).

Factors affecting PD: organic matter, mineral composition. Factors affecting BD: texture, organic matter, structure, compaction, tillage.

Volumetric flask method (procedure) ও soil suspension গরম করার কারণ 2.0
  1. Weigh the empty 100 mL volumetric flask (W).
  2. Add 20–30 g soil and weigh (W₁); weight of soil = W₁ − W.
  3. Add 30 mL water with a measuring cylinder.
  4. Heat for 15 minutes in a hot water bath.
  5. Cool to room temperature with tap water.
  6. Add 2–3 drops of alcohol and make the volume 100 mL.
  7. Weigh flask + soil + water (W₂) and record the temperature and density of water (Dw).

Volume of water = (W₂ − W₁) ÷ Dw. Volume of soil solids = 100 − volume of water. PD = mass of soil ÷ volume of soil solids.

Why heat: to disperse soil aggregates and expel trapped air, so the water fills all spaces.

Soil sampling: definition ও objectives (V.V.I) 2.0

Soil sampling: It is the process of collecting a small volume of soil from the field and its processing for subsequent mechanical and chemical analysis.

Objectives of soil sampling:

  • To measure soil nutrient status and lime requirement, in order to make fertilizer and lime recommendation for profitable farming.
  • To predict the probable crop response to applied nutrients.
  • To classify soils into different fertility groups for preparing soil fertility maps.
  • To identify the types and degree of soil related problems like salinity, alkalinity, acidity etc. and to suggest appropriate reclamation measures.

Depth and tool: 0–15 cm (0–6 inch) by auger.

লিখতে হবে: procedure, precautions ও result সবসময় past tense ও passive voice এ।

Particle density: নোটের অতিরিক্ত পয়েন্ট 1.0
  • Formula: PD = mass of soil ÷ volume of soil solids; density = mass ÷ volume; volume = mass ÷ density.
  • Volume of soil solids = 100 − volume of water; volume of water = (W₂ − W₁) ÷ Dw.
  • Temperature recorded to know the exact density of water (Dw) at that temperature.
  • Hot water bath: to disperse the soil aggregates; heating is also done to remove trapped air.
  • Range: mineral soil 2.60–2.75 g/cm³; organic soil 1.20–1.70 g/cm³. A value near 2.3 g/cm³ with mineral soil suggests a mechanical (experimental) error; above 2.75 indicates a heavy mineral soil.
Bulk density: নোটের অতিরিক্ত পয়েন্ট 1.0
  • BD = mass of oven-dry soil ÷ bulk volume of soil. Bulk volume = volume of soil solids + volume of pore space.
  • Methods (প্রশ্নে আসে): 1. Paraffined clod method, 2. Core sampler method.
  • Core sampler: volume of core = πr²h. Bulk volume of soil = internal volume of the core.
  • Values: mineral soil 1.0–1.8 g/cm³; organic soil about 0.5 g/cm³.
  • High BD (for example 1.6 g/cm³) means compact soil, so root growth is hampered and porosity decreases.
Rock ও mineral: নোটের অতিরিক্ত পয়েন্ট 1.0
  • Rock: mixture of minerals. Organic rock contains organic matter, example: coal. Coral limestone has a honeycomb-like structure.
  • Hydrated minerals (contain water): gypsum CaSO₄·2H₂O, hydrated alumina Al₂O₃·2H₂O, limonite Fe₂O₃·3H₂O.
  • Anhydrous minerals (no water): pyrite FeS₂, galena PbS.
  • Gypsum is a fertilizer mineral; it is a by-product of TSP.
Gram staining: নোটের অতিরিক্ত পয়েন্ট 1.0–2.0
  • Developed in 1884 by the Danish bacteriologist Hans Christian Gram.
  • Primary (basic) dye: crystal violet. Mordant: iodine solution. Decolourizing agent: ethyl alcohol. Counter stain: safranin.
  • Gram positive keeps the initial dye, so it looks violet. Gram negative takes safranin, so it looks red.
  • Reasons: (1) thickness of the peptidoglycan layer in Gram positive; (2) presence of an outer lipid membrane in Gram negative, and lipid dissolves in alcohol.
  • Mordant: to increase the affinity of the basic dye to the bacterial cell wall and to intensify the colour of the stain.
  • Smear: a heat-fixed thin layer of bacterial film.
Motility test (hanging drop) ও culture media 1.0–2.0
  • Brownian movement: only back and forth vibration. Vital (true) movement: by flagella.
  • Non-motile bacteria cannot move because of loss of flagella or their physiological condition.
  • Hollow ground slide is used for the hanging drop method.
  • Media for bacterial culture: YMB (Yeast Mannitol Broth, liquid) and YMA (Yeast Mannitol Agar, solid). Mannitol = carbohydrate source; yeast = amino acids, growth factors, vitamin B complex; agar = solidifying agent.
  • Moist heat is better than dry heat due to rapid penetration of heat in a moist environment, so less time is needed. Autoclave: 121 °C, 15 psi, 15–30 minutes.
