Privacy Policy

AGRi ELITE NOTES (https://agri-elite-arena.netlify.app) is a free study-notes website and app for HSTU Agriculture students, made by Mahfuz R. Shanto.

What we collect

  • Anonymous usage statistics (page visits and button clicks) through GoatCounter. It does not use cookies and does not identify you.
  • Built-in visit counter: records only the page address, device type (phone, tablet or desktop) and the referring website. No cookies, no IP address, no personal data. It is skipped if your browser sends Do Not Track.
  • Optional student account (student portal): if you register we store your name, email, optional study details you enter (university, department, year, semester), your saved notes, quiz results, flashcard progress and study tasks. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and are never visible to us. Your data is visible only to you and the site administrators.
  • Feedback form: if you submit it, we receive your rating, your message and the name only if you choose to write it. Nothing else is required.
  • On your device only: your theme choice, "finished reading" ticks and best self-test score are saved in your browser or app storage. We never receive them.

What we do not do

We do not ask for phone numbers or payment details. A password is needed only if you create a student account. We do not sell or share your data and we show no ads.

Children

The app is meant for university students.

Contact

Use the feedback form on the website or the Facebook link in the footer.
